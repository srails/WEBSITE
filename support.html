Frequently asked

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Email us at siriusappdevelopment@gmail.com with a short description. Screenshots and your device/OS version help us resolve issues faster.

Which devices does Omey Tide support?

Omey Tide runs on current iOS devices. For the best experience, please keep the app updated to the latest version from the App Store.
